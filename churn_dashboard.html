Recommendation: the highest-risk segment is new customers (0–12 months) on month-to-month contracts with fiber internet, paying by electronic check, without tech support or online security add-ons. Prioritizing this segment for annual-contract incentives, autopay enrollment, and bundled support offers is likely to have the largest impact on retention.
